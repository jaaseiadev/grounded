# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

Effective January 1, 2026

This handbook describes the workplace practices of Northstar Labs, a fictional software studio. It is sample content created for testing Grounded and is not legal advice.

## Working Hours and Flexible Scheduling

Northstar Labs uses a flexible workday. Employees may begin between 7:30 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. in their local time zone, provided they are available during the collaboration window from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Team members are expected to record planned schedule changes on the shared calendar before the start of the affected workday.

Full-time employees normally work 40 hours each week. Managers may approve compressed schedules when customer coverage and project commitments remain satisfied. A compressed schedule must be documented in writing and reviewed every three months. Employees should not routinely work beyond their agreed hours; recurring workload concerns should be raised during one-on-one meetings.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely from an approved location. The company provides a one-time equipment allowance of $900 for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Purchases must be supported by receipts and submitted within 60 days of the employee's start date. The allowance does not cover recurring internet or utility charges.

Work involving confidential customer information must be performed on company-managed devices. Devices must use full-disk encryption, automatic screen locking, and the company virtual private network when connected to public Wi-Fi. Lost devices must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery.

Employees who want to work from a different country for more than ten business days must request approval at least 30 days in advance. Approval depends on payroll, tax, data residency, and customer contract requirements. A manager's verbal approval is not sufficient.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off each calendar year. New employees receive a prorated balance based on their start date. Up to five unused days may carry into the following calendar year; any additional unused balance expires on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise.

Requests for one or two days of leave should normally be submitted at least three business days in advance. Requests for three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Managers may make exceptions for emergencies. Employees must arrange a handoff for urgent work and identify a point of contact before extended leave.

Northstar Labs also provides ten paid sick days per year. Sick leave may be used for the employee's illness, medical appointment, preventive care, or care of an immediate family member. A medical diagnosis should never be included in a public team channel. Human Resources may request limited documentation for absences longer than five consecutive workdays where permitted by law.

## Professional Development

Each full-time employee has an annual professional development budget of $1,500. Eligible expenses include courses, certifications, technical books, and conferences connected to the employee's role or growth plan. Manager approval is required before purchase. Conference travel is charged to the same budget unless a vice president approves separate project funding.

Employees who complete a course should share useful materials or a short summary with their team. The budget does not roll over between calendar years and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Information Security

Confidential information includes customer data, credentials, nonpublic financial results, product roadmaps, security findings, and personal employee information. Access must follow the principle of least privilege. Employees may not copy confidential information into personal email, consumer file-sharing services, or unapproved AI tools.

Suspected phishing, accidental disclosure, or unauthorized access must be reported immediately through the security incident channel. Employees should preserve relevant messages and should not attempt to investigate an incident on their own. The security team coordinates containment and communication.

Passwords must be unique and stored in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication is required wherever supported. Shared credentials are prohibited unless stored in an approved team vault with access logging.

## Performance and Feedback

Managers hold quarterly growth conversations covering outcomes, collaboration, skills, and support needed. The annual performance review summarizes those conversations but should not introduce significant concerns for the first time. Employees may add written context to their review within seven calendar days.

When performance does not meet expectations, the manager should explain the specific gap, the expected outcome, available support, and a reasonable review date. Formal improvement plans are coordinated with Human Resources. Nothing in this section changes the at-will employment relationship where that relationship is permitted by law.

## Workplace Conduct

Northstar Labs expects respectful, inclusive, and professional behavior in physical and digital workplaces. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, and deliberate intimidation are prohibited. Concerns may be raised with a manager, Human Resources, or the confidential ethics channel. Employees do not need to report a concern through their direct manager first.

Reports are reviewed promptly and shared only with people who need the information to assess and respond. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.
