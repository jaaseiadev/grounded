# Alder & Finch Product Return Policy

Effective March 15, 2026

Alder & Finch is a fictional home-goods retailer. This original policy is provided as sample data for Grounded.

## Standard Returns

Customers may return unused products within 30 calendar days after delivery. The product must be in resalable condition and include its original packaging, manuals, accessories, and proof of purchase. A return authorization must be requested before the product is shipped back. Packages sent without an authorization may take longer to identify and process.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. After the return reaches the warehouse, inspection normally takes three to five business days. The payment provider may require an additional two to seven business days to post the credit. Original express-shipping fees are not refundable unless Alder & Finch shipped the wrong item or the product arrived damaged.

Customers are responsible for standard return shipping when a correctly supplied product is unwanted or no longer needed. Alder & Finch provides a prepaid label for incorrect, defective, or damaged products.

## Damaged Products

Visible shipping damage should be reported within 72 hours after delivery. The customer should provide the order number and clear photographs of the outer packaging, shipping label, damaged area, and all included parts. Packaging should be retained until the support team confirms that it is no longer needed.

For a verified shipping-damage claim, the customer may choose a replacement at no additional charge or a full refund. If the item is no longer available, Alder & Finch will issue a full refund. Replacement orders use standard shipping unless the support team approves an expedited method because of a time-sensitive event.

Damage discovered after unpacking but within the 30-day return window is reviewed under the same process. Normal wear, accidental damage after delivery, misuse, and damage caused by unsupported installation are not covered as shipping damage. These exclusions do not limit any rights provided by applicable consumer law.

Large furniture does not need to be shipped before the support team reviews the evidence. Alder & Finch may arrange an inspection, parts shipment, repair visit, donation, collection, or local disposal. Customers should not discard large items until written instructions are provided.

## Defective Products and Warranty

A product that fails during normal use within 30 days may be returned for a replacement or refund. After 30 days, the manufacturer's warranty applies when one is included with the product. Support can provide warranty contact information and proof of purchase, but the manufacturer determines warranty eligibility.

If troubleshooting could safely resolve the issue, support may ask the customer to complete a short diagnostic process. Customers are never required to disassemble electrical components or perform a step that creates a safety risk.

## Final-Sale and Nonreturnable Items

Gift cards, personalized products, opened hygiene products, and items clearly marked final sale cannot be returned for preference-based reasons. A final-sale designation does not prevent a customer from reporting an item that arrived damaged, was supplied incorrectly, or is covered by mandatory consumer protections.

Mattresses may be returned only under the separate 100-night comfort trial described on the product page. The comfort trial requires at least 30 nights of use and applies once per household in a 12-month period. Delivery, collection, or sanitation charges disclosed at checkout may be deducted from a comfort-trial refund.

## Exchanges

A direct exchange is available only for a different size or color of the same product when inventory is available. The replacement is reserved after the carrier scans the return package. Price differences caused by a later promotion are not applied to an exchange.

For a different product, the original item is returned for a refund and the customer places a new order. Support cannot transfer discounts that have expired, but a documented pricing error will be reviewed.

## Return Shipping and Packaging

Small parcels should be packed securely to prevent movement in transit. Customers should remove or cover unrelated shipping labels and attach the return label to the largest flat surface. Products containing batteries must follow the carrier instructions included with the label.

When Alder & Finch provides a prepaid label, it must be used within 14 calendar days. An expired label may be replaced if the original 30-day return request was timely. Customers should retain the carrier receipt until the refund or replacement is complete.

## Refund Exceptions and Review

A refund may be reduced when nonessential accessories or original packaging are missing and the omission reduces the product's resale value. No deduction is made for packaging opened solely to inspect the product. Support must explain any proposed deduction before the refund is finalized.

Returns showing signs of fraud, material substitution, or intentional damage may be escalated for review. Escalation does not automatically deny the return. The customer will receive a written decision, normally within ten business days, and may request a second review by replying within 14 calendar days.

## How to Start a Return

Customers start a return through the order-history page or by contacting support. The request should include the order number, item, reason for return, and preferred resolution. Support sends the authorization, packing guidance, and shipping instructions by email.

Questions about an existing return should reference the return authorization number. This helps support locate warehouse scans, inspection notes, replacement orders, and refund status without requesting information the customer has already provided.
